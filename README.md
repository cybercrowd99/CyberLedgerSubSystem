# CyberAnalyticsSubSystem

## 0. Repository Identity

Purpose:
Define the analytical observation organ of CyberCrowd.

Scope:
Signal geometry analysis only.

Status:
Skeleton — boundary validation phase.

---

# 1. Subsystem Identity

CyberAnalyticsSubSystem is the analytical observation organ of the CyberCrowd reactor.

CyberAnalytics exists to observe structural geometry without collapsing signals into human identity.

CyberAnalytics analyzes relationships between signals.

CyberAnalytics does not analyze people.

---

# 2. Subsystem Purpose

CyberAnalytics preserves understanding of:

- signal relationships
- contribution geometry
- structural topology
- continuity patterns
- evidence relationships
- system integrity conditions

CyberAnalytics does not preserve:

- human profiles
- operator histories
- behavioral records
- personal preferences
- identity trajectories

Analytics describes the field.

Analytics does not own the source.

---

# 3. Core Boundary Invariant

## Analytics ≠ Surveillance

CyberAnalytics must observe signal geometry, not human identity.

Required:

- analytics must remain blind to identity payloads
- analytics must remain blind to operator behavior
- analytics must remain blind to personal history
- analytics must remain blind to preference patterns
- analytics must remain blind to identity correlation
- analytics must remain blind to trajectory inference

Forbidden:

- collapsing signals into identity
- deriving identity from continuity
- predicting human behavior
- ranking operators
- prescribing actions
- generating authority

Invariant:

Signal → Geometry

Never →

Person

- a reputation engine
- a behavior engine
- a human classification system
